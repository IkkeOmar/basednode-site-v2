Frequently Asked Questions

What does BasedNode do?+
BasedNode is my solo operation. I help businesses automate workflows, build AI agents, create websites and apps, generate leads, and reduce costs through smart technology. Every project gets my direct attention — no account managers, no bureaucracy.
How does revenue share work?+
For many projects, I offer a revenue share model where you pay a reduced upfront fee plus a percentage of the value generated. This aligns incentives — I only do well when you do. Typical revenue shares range from 10-20%.
What's your background?+
I'm studying to be a Civilingeniør in Physics & Nanotechnology from DTU, with a background in quant finance. I've been teaching math, programming & AI since 2019 (MentorDanmark) and AI at GXU since 2025. I've built software since 2020 and achieved 80-100x cost reductions on AI infrastructure.
How long does a project take?+
A simple website takes 1-2 weeks. A custom app or automation workflow takes 3-6 weeks. Larger full-scale solutions are discussed and scoped together. I work iteratively so you see results as we go.
Can a non-technical person work with you?+
Absolutely. That is actually my specialty. I bridge the gap between what you need and the technology to deliver it. You focus on your business, I handle the technical side. No jargon, no surprises — just results.

Ready to get started?

I work with businesses of all sizes. The first conversation is always free and always low-pressure. Just reach out and we will figure out if it makes sense.
