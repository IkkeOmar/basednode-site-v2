frequently asked questions

What's the typical timeline for a project? +
Most projects take 2-4 weeks from kickoff to deployment. Smaller automation tasks can be delivered in under a week, while larger full-stack AI products may take 6-8 weeks. I prioritize iterating fast and delivering value early.
How does the revenue share model work? +
For suitable projects, I offer a reduced upfront fee in exchange for a percentage of measurable value created — typically 12-20% of cost savings or new revenue. Terms are agreed upfront with clear tracking metrics.
Do you offer maintenance after launch? +
Yes. Retainer-based maintenance starts at €500/month and includes monitoring, minor updates, and priority bug fixes. Growth and Partnership tiers include bundled maintenance periods.
What technologies do you work with? +
Python, PyTorch, LangChain, React/Next.js, FastAPI, Docker, PostgreSQL, Qdrant/Chroma vector DBs, and major LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama). I pick the right tool for each problem.
Can you work with my existing team? +
Absolutely. I embed directly into your workflow — Slack, Notion, GitHub, or whatever tools you use. I collaborate closely with your engineers and stakeholders for seamless integration and knowledge transfer.
